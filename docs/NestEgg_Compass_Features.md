# NestEgg Compass — Product Concept & Feature Blueprint

## Why this product matters
NestEgg Compass is designed for people who often feel financially “on their own”:
- Students in a new city/country.
- Newly married or new families managing household costs abroad.
- Friends sharing expenses during travel.

It gives confidence, transparency, and fairness in personal and group money management.

## Key Value Propositions
1. **Simple daily tracking** for income and expenses.
2. **Strong group split tools** for trips and shared activities.
3. **Student and family friendly categories** out of the box.
4. **Attractive, stylish UI** that users enjoy returning to.

## Feature List (MVP + Future)

### A) Account & Onboarding
- Email/social login.
- Onboarding wizard (country, currency, monthly target, use-case selection).
- Starter templates: Student Budget, New Family Budget, Travel Group.

### B) Personal Finance Tracking
- Add income/expense with category, date, note, receipt image.
- Recurring entries (rent, subscriptions, salary).
- Payment modes (cash, card, wallet, transfer).
- Search and filters by date, category, amount.

### C) Budgeting & Alerts
- Monthly and category-level budgets.
- Real-time budget progress bars.
- Alert tiers at 60%, 85%, and 100% budget usage.
- “Safe to spend” estimate for remaining days in month.

### D) Money Split (Core Differentiator)
- Create split groups: Trip, Flatmates, Event.
- Add members and shared expenses.
- Split options:
  - Equal
  - Custom amount per member
  - Percentage-based
- Track paid/unpaid status.
- Auto-settlement suggestions to minimize number of payments.
- Group timeline for expense history.

### E) Reports & Insights
- Weekly/monthly spending charts.
- Top spending categories.
- Income vs expense trend.
- Savings rate graph.
- Group-wise total spend and member balances.

### F) Collaboration
- Household shared board (partner/family mode).
- Comment on group transactions.
- Optional approval flow for high-value shared expenses.

### G) Localization & Accessibility
- Multi-currency support.
- Localization-ready labels/date formats.
- Accessible color contrast and keyboard navigation.

### H) Future Enhancements
- AI suggestions (spending anomalies, savings tips).
- Bank/SMS transaction import.
- Goal-based savings jars (education, travel, emergency fund).
- Bill reminders and autopay tracking.

## Suggested Information Architecture
- **Dashboard**
- **Transactions**
- **Budgets**
- **Groups & Split**
- **Reports**
- **Settings**

## Suggested Tech Direction (Web app, mobile-ready)
- Responsive web app (PWA first), later native/hybrid app.
- REST/GraphQL backend.
- Secure auth + encrypted data handling.
- Scalable event logging for analytics.

## Visual Style Direction
- Modern minimal UI with soft gradients and clean cards.
- Friendly typography with clear hierarchy.
- Color system:
  - Green for income/savings
  - Warm tones for expenses
  - Blue/purple accents for shared/group activities

## Launch Positioning Statement
**“NestEgg Compass helps students, young families, and travel groups take control of money with easy tracking, smart budgeting, and stress-free split settlements.”**
